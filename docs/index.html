IHIG

Four small games. They run in the browser — nothing to install.

Tessera

a puzzle in four spatial dimensions

You stand in a three dimensional slice of a four dimensional room, so the walls you can see are only the walls in this slice. Step along the axis you cannot see and the wall in front of you may simply not be there. Turn that axis into view and a wall one cell thick becomes a corridor running away from you.

Chambers
43, each proved solvable and given a par by exhaustive search
Controls
on-screen pad on a phone, WASD and Q/E/F/G on a keyboard
Best on
a wide screen — it is a landscape game
Play in the browser Download for Android

Lumen

turn the mirrors, light every ring

A few sources fire beams across a grid and a few rings are waiting to be lit, each in a particular colour. Between them sit mirrors you can turn. Two beams arriving at the same ring mix — red and green make yellow, and a ring asking for yellow will not take anything else, so working out which two have to meet there is most of the game.

Levels
endless, and every one is built backwards from its own solution
Controls
one thumb — tap a mirror to turn it
Pressure
none. Nothing is timed and nothing can be lost
Play in the browser

Planet Hopper

hop from orbit to orbit, and keep climbing

You fly a craft that climbs endlessly upward. Two meters decide how far you get: fuel, which you spend steering between planets, and oxygen, which drains the whole time and only refills in the orbit of a green world. Oxygen is the clock — you are never allowed to settle anywhere for long.

Run
endless, and it gets meaner the higher you climb
Controls
one thumb — tap to break orbit, hold to steer
Preview
a dotted line shows where a launch will actually take you
Play in the browser

Dunk Rush

aim the jump, beat the shot clock

You hang from a rim and jump to the next basket up. Drag back to aim and a dotted line shows you exactly where you will land — it bounces off backboards, so banking is a shot you can aim on purpose, and it turns red and stops where the line would hit iron. The shot clock resets only when you score, so there is no parking on a rim to line up the perfect jump.

Run
endless — the rims get narrower and start sliding as you climb
Controls
one thumb — drag back to aim, hold in the air to hang
Scoring
swishes and bank shots pay extra; clipping the rim costs the combo
Play in the browser

The first game you open takes a moment. All four are built on the same engine, and that engine is a single large download shared between them — so whichever one you try first pays for it, and the rest then start almost at once. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
