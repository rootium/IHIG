TESSERA

a puzzle game in four spatial dimensions

You are standing in a three dimensional slice of a four dimensional room. The walls you can see are only the walls in this slice. Step along the axis you cannot see and a wall in front of you may simply not be there; turn that axis into view and a wall one cell thick becomes a corridor running away from you.

Forty-three chambers. Every one proved solvable by exhaustive search before it shipped, and its shortest solution is the par you are playing against.

Play

Play in the browser → Works on desktop and phone. No install. Android APK ↓ 26 MB · arm64 · Android 7.0+ · sideload

Controls

WASD move · Q E kata and ana, the two directions along the hidden axis · F G turn a visible axis into the hidden one · Z undo · R restart · drag to orbit the camera. On a phone the same moves are on screen.

Desktop builds

Windows and Linux binaries are a single command from a clone — they are a hundred megabytes each, which is more than a git repository should carry:

tools/build.sh games/tessera linux windows

The APK is signed with a self-signed key, so Android will warn you when you sideload it. That is expected: it is not a Play Store release.
